# Ten questions for teacher approval

Review wording and hints before approving any audio. These are the current canonical questions in `src/app/interview.models.ts`. Ten MP3 files are present in `public/audio/questions/`, but their wording and approval status have not been verified in this review; no new audio was generated during this correction.

## Q1 · Transition to Secondary School

**Question:** What has been the biggest change for you since starting secondary school?

**Hint (shown on request):** Think of one change you have noticed in your school day.

## Q2 · Transition to Secondary School

**Question:** What helps you stay organised during the school day?

**Hint (shown on request):** Think of a routine or tool you use yourself.

## Q3 · Transition to Secondary School

**Question:** Tell me about something that felt difficult at first. What helped you manage it?

**Hint (shown on request):** Choose a small challenge and what you did next.

## Q4 · Favourite Subjects

**Question:** Which school subject do you enjoy most, and why?

**Hint (shown on request):** Think about what you like doing or learning in that subject.

## Q5 · Favourite Subjects

**Question:** Can you describe a lesson, activity or piece of work in that subject that you enjoyed or felt proud of?

**Hint (shown on request):** Recall one real lesson or piece of work.

## Q6 · Extra-Curricular Activities

**Question:** Which clubs, sports or other activities do you enjoy in school or outside school?

**Hint (shown on request):** An activity at home counts too. It is fine if you have not joined one.

## Q7 · Extra-Curricular Activities

**Question:** What have you learnt from an activity? If you have not joined one yet, what would you like to try, and why?

**Hint (shown on request):** Think about a skill you have learnt or an activity you would like to explore.

## Q8 · Leadership and Responsibility

**Question:** Tell me about a time you helped someone or worked well with a team.

**Hint (shown on request):** A small, everyday act of help counts.

## Q9 · Leadership and Responsibility

**Question:** What makes a good leader, and how do you try to show those qualities?

**Hint (shown on request):** Think about listening, helping or taking responsibility.

## Q10 · Leadership and Responsibility

**Question:** What is one responsibility or small leadership role you would like to take on at school?

**Hint (shown on request):** Choose a small action you could realistically try.
