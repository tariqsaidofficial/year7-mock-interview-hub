# Approved Year 7 questions and rubric V2

Question 1 remains byte-for-byte unchanged in the application. IDs and order remain 1–10. The hints are screen-only guidance; they are not added to the recorded audio.

| ID | Domain | Exact question | Screen hint |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Q1 | Transition & Adjustment | What has been the biggest change for you since starting secondary school? | Think of one change you have noticed in your school day. |
| Q2 | Transition & Adjustment | How have you found the transition from primary to secondary school? What has been the biggest change for you so far? | Think about one change and how it has felt for you. |
| Q3 | Learning & Curiosity | What is your favourite subject at school and why? What do you enjoy most about learning it? | Think of something you enjoy learning or doing in that subject. |
| Q4 | Relationships & Leadership | What does being a good leader mean to you? Can you give an example of a time when you have shown leadership, either in or outside school? | A small moment when you helped or guided others counts. |
| Q5 | Relationships & Leadership | What do you think makes someone a good friend and a good team member? How do you contribute when working with others? | Think about listening, including others or sharing a task. |
| Q6 | Growth & Aspirations | What has been your proudest achievement so far? It could be something academic, sporting, creative or personal. | Choose something that mattered to you and say why. |
| Q7 | Growth & Aspirations | What do you do when you find something difficult or make a mistake? Can you give an example of how you overcome a challenge? | Think of one challenge and what you tried next. |
| Q8 | Growth & Aspirations | If you could improve one thing about yourself this academic year, what would it be and why? | Choose one skill or habit you would like to work on. |
| Q9 | Learning & Curiosity | What new skill would you like to learn or develop during your time at secondary school? How might this help you in the future? | Think about a skill you are curious to try and how it could help. |
| Q10 | Growth & Aspirations | Where do you see yourself in five years' time? What would you like to have achieved by then? | Share one hope for the future in your own words. |

New completed records carry `rubricVersion: 2`. Existing records without a version retain the original question text and four original category names in the teacher view. An older local draft remains copyable with its original question wording, but cannot resume or submit against V2 questions. Completed V1 results remain readable.
